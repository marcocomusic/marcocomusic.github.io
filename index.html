Bio

A decade on the decks.
A new chapter.

Rooted in house music and backed by more than a decade behind the decks, Marcoco has shaped a style of his own — built on diverse influences and a natural instinct to keep the dancefloor moving.

Since 2013 he has been one half of the house duo C'mon, playing across Europe, South America and Asia, with releases on established house labels along the way. From 2022 to 2025 he also co-ran the record label PRïMA.

In 2025 it was time for a solo project. As Marcoco, his selections move freely between house, techno and beyond — driven by curiosity for different genres and a love for the spaces where musical boundaries blur. His sets weave old-school influences, rolling grooves, driving rhythms, sweet harmonies and unexpected twists into a sound that shifts between moods and energies without ever losing its drive.
